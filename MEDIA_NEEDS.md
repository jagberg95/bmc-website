# Bar Moon Contracting — Media Needs List

**Goal:** Real, high-quality photos and videos that showcase actual work. Prioritize before/after, jobsite details, finished results, and local Central Texas context. Avoid generic stock.

**Recommended File Naming Convention** (use this for everything new):
`bmc_[category]_[town]_[description]_[01].jpg`

Examples:
- `bmc_custom-home_salado_exterior_01.jpg`
- `bmc_renovation_temple_kitchen_before.jpg`
- `bmc_outdoor_killeen_patio_finished_02.jpg`
- `bmc_process_framing_belton_01.jpg`

**Formats:** JPG or WebP (high resolution). Shoot in good light. Include people when appropriate (clients, crew) with permission.
